    Study Assistant

    A quiet place to study your own notes

    Create a subject, add your lecture notes, papers, slides, photos or recordings, then ask questions about them or let the assistant quiz you.

    1. Add material. PDFs are read directly; images go through a vision model and audio through Whisper.
    2. Ask. Answers are written from the passages that match your question, and every answer shows where it came from.
    3. Practise. Quiz questions are written from your documents and get harder or easier as you answer.

    Answers come only from this subject's documents. Enter to ask · Shift+Enter for a new line ·
